About Us

The Heart of
Assam Cricket

Founded on September 21, 2022, Assam Cricket Club was established with a singular vision — to create a unified home for cricketers, technical personnel, office bearers, and sports enthusiasts of the region.

Situated inside the magnificent ACA Stadium at Barsapara, Guwahati, our club is more than just a cricket facility. It is a vibrant community that nurtures sports, culture, and meaningful human connections. Every corner of the club reflects the rich sporting heritage of Assam.
